How a job travels

Send a message with Orchestrate. A job ends as reviewed branches, and merging stays with you.

YOU
Message
Direct goes to the conversation as always. Auto or Orchestrate can make it a job.
→
1
Triage
Says what it understood, asks what is unclear, and sizes the work.
→
2
Plan
One planner for small work. For big work, a parliament discusses until one plan stands.
→
3
Steps
The plan is divided. Each step owns its files and knows what it waits for.
→
4
Build
An agent per step, side by side, each in its own worktree and branch.
→
5
Review
A different agent reviews each step. Findings go back, up to three rounds.
→
6
Final read
The chair reads the whole job: done, or a question for you on its card.
The boardEvery agent sees the plan, the steps and each other's notes.
OversightChecks at the end of every turn. A step that leaves its own files is paused.
Extra headsA split parliament asks outside advisors, then you. Nobody votes.
